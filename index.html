📐 Unit Circle Cheat Sheet & Family Patterns

🌟 ASTC Quadrant Rule (Signs)

  • Q1 (All): All trig functions are Positive
  • Q2 (Students): Only Sin / Csc are Positive
  • Q3 (Take): Only Tan / Cot are Positive
  • Q4 (Calculus): Only Cos / Sec are Positive

🔄 Reciprocal Family Patterns

  • Csc(x) = 1 / Sin(x)
  • Sec(x) = 1 / Cos(x)
  • Cot(x) = 1 / Tan(x) = Cos(x) / Sin(x)
  • Tan(x) = Sin(x) / Cos(x)

⚡ Standard Angle Families

  • π/6 (30°) Family: Sin=1/2, Cos=√3/2, Tan=√3/3
  • π/4 (45°) Family: Sin=√2/2, Cos=√2/2, Tan=1
  • π/3 (60°) Family: Sin=√3/2, Cos=1/2, Tan=√3

📍 Quadrantal Angles

  • 0 / 2π: Sin=0, Cos=1, Tan=0, Sec=1, Csc=und
  • π/2 (90°): Sin=1, Cos=0, Tan=und, Sec=und
  • π (180°): Sin=0, Cos=-1, Tan=0, Sec=-1
  • 3π/2 (270°): Sin=-1, Cos=0, Tan=und, Sec=und
